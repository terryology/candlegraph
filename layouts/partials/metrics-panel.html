Reading the numbers

SAVScent Adjusted Value

How much scent performance a candle delivers — throw, complexity, longevity — indexed so {{ site.Params.baseline }} is the collection average. Higher is stronger.

PAEPrice-Adjusted Efficiency

What that performance costs you. Indexed to {{ site.Params.baseline }}; higher means more scent per dollar. The Diptyque scores low here — it's worth it, not cheap.

{{ site.Params.baseline }} = average of every candle scored to date.
